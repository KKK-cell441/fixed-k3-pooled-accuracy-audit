Dear Editor,

\vspace{0.4em}
Please consider our manuscript, ``Pooled Accuracy Is Not Enough to Authorize
Fixed-K3 VMD Preprocessing: Same-Source and External Bearing Audits,'' for
publication in \emph{Measurement}.

The manuscript addresses a measurement-evaluation problem: a preprocessing
decision is often authorized by pooled diagnostic accuracy even when
measurement conditions, validation units, and class-specific effects can change
what that number means. The paper's contribution is an empirical audit of this
authorization problem, not a new VMD algorithm or classifier.

The first central result is a same-source HUSTbearing audit. Against a
clean-trained raw baseline, fixed K3 shows a speed-level gain of 9.72
percentage points. When both raw and K3 are trained with matched 0 dB noise
augmentation, the effect reverses to -7.94 points (unit-only interval
[-12.61, -3.44]; crossed robustness interval [-12.72, -3.28]). The
matched-augmentation audit is Not qualified under an illustrative materiality
margin of 1 point. The clean-trained advantage is therefore baseline-specific,
not a deployment warrant.

The second central result is an independent PHM2012/PRONOSTIA run-to-failure
audit with 17 non-fragile physical bearings. Clean-trained K3 raises pooled
balanced accuracy by 24.51 points, but the healthy class improves by 70.75
points while the degraded class falls by 21.73 points (simultaneous interval
[-43.14, -5.39]). The block is therefore Not qualified on class-harm grounds
despite its positive pooled mean. Matched augmentation changes the mean effect
to -6.29 points. This external block makes the central measurement point
visible: a pooled improvement can coexist with class-specific harm.

The study is a secondary analysis of completed vibration experiments and adds
no physical test campaign. Additional HSE, HUST-Hanoi, SCA, and Mehran-UET
audits remain Insufficient or Not qualified under the same evidentiary
discipline. A three-state evidence audit is used to distinguish Not qualified
from Insufficient and to prevent a missing or fragile class interval from being
read as a pass. This audit is a decision aid, not a validated certification
framework, and no real block receives Qualified in the present evidence.
CCMG is reported only as an optional measurement-condition prefilter with small
incremental value.

The work directly addresses two areas in the journal's scope: evaluation
procedures for performance analysis of measurement processes and algorithms,
and measurement data processing. The cover letter does not position the paper
as a new sensor-calibration method or a new fault-diagnosis network.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all
authors will approve the submitted version. The analysis code, frozen
protocols, generated tables, figures, and result files are available at
https://github.com/KKK-cell441/fixed-k3-pooled-accuracy-audit. The source
datasets retain their original licenses and access conditions.
